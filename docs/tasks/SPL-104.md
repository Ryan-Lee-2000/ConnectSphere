# SPL-104 — Be notified of new booking requests

Source: [Jira SPL-104](https://clivelim01-1787647390567.atlassian.net/browse/SPL-104).
Estimate: 2 points. Sprint 4, epic CS-E20 Notification System.

As a Venue Staff member, I want to be notified in-app and by email when a venue booking request is
submitted or withdrawn so that I can review it promptly.

## Acceptance criteria

- AC1. Notified when a venue booking request is submitted (W1 §6: "A venue booking is requested")
- AC2. Notified when a venue booking request is withdrawn. Under Q110 a change to a request is made
  by withdrawing it and submitting a new one, so AC1 and AC2 together cover a booking being
  "changed" (W1 §6)
- AC3. Sent to all Venue Staff; access is not restricted by venue (Q66). Recipients are chosen by
  the roles assigned to each account: an account holding several roles is notified for each role
  that applies, including when it submitted the request itself (Q56; PO decision)
- AC4. Each venue booking gets its own notice. An event with several venue bookings produces one
  notice per booking, and withdrawing one has no effect on the others' notices (W7 #3; PO decision)
- AC5. The notice includes all relevant booking details, clearly formatted: event, venue, date,
  exact Singapore start/end times in 15-minute units (Q128) and the setup/turnaround occupancy
  (W7 #1), taken from the booking's exact timing
- AC6. Out of scope for this story: email delivery of these notices is built and verified by
  SPL-125 (its AC5 covers SPL-101-104). Approved by the team, 24 Oct 2026 (previously "The notice
  is also sent by email under SPL-125")
- AC7. A refused or failed submission or withdrawal sends no notice, and retrying an action does
  not create a duplicate notice

Source: Week 1 §6; Week 4 core #20; Week 7 #1, #3; Q56, Q66, Q110, Q128. Team decisions by the PO on
24 October 2026: triggers are limited to submitted and withdrawn; one notice per venue booking;
notify by role, including the account that submitted the request.

## Approved implementation decisions (Ranveer, 24 October 2026)

- Every SPL-104 notice opens `/workspace/booking-requests`, the Venue Staff booking queue. Venue
  Staff cannot open the coordinator event page. The frontend accepts that link alongside the
  existing pages. A withdrawn request leaves the queue, so the message carries every detail itself.
- Recipients are active Venue Staff accounts (`accounts.is_active`), as in SPL-122.
- Messages name only the event, venue, date, Singapore times and occupancy. No layout, attendee
  count or requester. For example, in 15-minute mode: "Venue booking requested for Founders Day:
  Hall A on Sat 14 Nov 2026, 10:00–12:00 SGT; venue occupied 09:30–12:45 including setup and
  turnaround." With `EXACT_VENUE_TIMING_ENABLED` off, bookings store whole slots, so the notice
  shows the event's times and the occupied slots: "…13:00–18:00 SGT; venue occupied PM (setup AM,
  turnaround Night)." A withdrawal starts "Venue booking request withdrawn for …".
- Occupancy that crosses midnight names the other day.
- Duplicates are stopped by the existing unique key on (recipient, dedupe key). The key is
  `venue_booking_requested:<booking id>` or `venue_booking_withdrawn:<booking id>`, so each booking
  has its own notice. Each notice is written in the action's own transaction.
- Out of scope: email (SPL-125), approval and rejection notices (SPL-121), cancelling a booking or
  an event (not a withdrawal), flagged bookings, venue unavailability and hold expiry (their own
  stories).
